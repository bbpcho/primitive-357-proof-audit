## Sufficient support, including the corrected 29-place

`checks/support/check_macaulay_support.py` constructs the correct degree-seven Macaulay system for three cubic partial derivatives. There are 36 degree-seven monomials. Each is divisible by a cube and is assigned to its first such cube; the nine monomials divisible by two cubes give the extraneous minor. The determinant quotient is the classical resultant. The division by 2^14 is the standard normalization for a ternary quartic, but even an uncertainty in that dyadic normalization would not affect the asserted support outside the already included dyadic places.

The source proves that the chosen scalar multiple has integral coefficients and coefficient ideal one. Where the partial resultant is a unit, its reduction has no common projective zero. Outside characteristic two, Euler's identity for a quartic then proves the primitive projective curve is smooth. A smooth proper plane model gives good reduction of its Jacobian, hence Tamagawa number one there. This is a sufficient bad-place set; it need not be a minimal one. It is substantially stronger than reading the number-field discriminant alone.

The source reconstructs the polynomial from the support manifest rather than parsing the manuscript directly. I closed that textual binding by comparing the 15 printed `(U,W)` pairs at manuscript lines 1347 onward with the manifest's exact rational pairs. The result gives exactly the two dyadic places and the places over 3,5,7 and **(29,s−15)**. The new actual-prime inventory verifies the four L-primes with indices 2,3,7,8 lie over this place. No conjugate 29-prime needs to be added merely because the stale prose labels it incorrectly.

## Odd class numbers and the analytic contradiction

If the **ordinary** class number of T is even, class field theory gives an everywhere-unramified quadratic extension in the ordinary Hilbert class field. The real places split as well: this is the ordinary, not narrow, class field. Thus the hypothetical field has degree 42, six real embeddings, and discriminant |disc(T)|². Every principal T-prime splits. The source `verify_t21_prime_generators_norm30000_v1.gp` freshly enumerates all prime ideals up to norm 30000, checks the exact model and discriminant, and verifies each principal ideal by HNF, element norm, factorization and multiplication back. The evidence is not an assumed BNF class number or a short list of convenient principal primes.

I inspected the directed Arb source. Its inequality direction is correct: it uses an upper enclosure for the subtracted integral and interval enclosures for the retained nonnegative local terms. The first cell follows from `1−f(z)≤z²/5`; the two tail bounds follow from the elementary hyperbolic integrals. The identity `g(z)=3 integral_0^1 t² sinc(zt)dt` gives |g|≤1 and hence 0≤f≤1, validating the interval intersection. The alternating series brackets g for |z|≤2, and the interval cell computation encloses ranges rather than approximating them by midpoint values. Omitting unlisted primes and higher local terms weakens the required lower discriminant bound. The sealed positive margin greater than 0.04072 therefore contradicts the hypothetical discriminant under the explicitly imported unconditional Brueggeman–Doud inequality.

This pass did not repeat the 262144-cell calculation. Its mathematical implementation and previously replayed exact input/output binding were reviewed; the report accurately distinguishes those facts from the new small support computations.

For F/T and N/F, the ordinary ambiguous class-number formula is being used with the correct index `E_base : E_base intersect Norm(extension*)`. It is not the generally different index of norms of extension units. At unramified finite places, units have no norm obstruction. At the ramified real places the sign contributes the missing obstruction, and the F/T source certifies those signs with Sturm intervals. The three independent character rows together with reciprocity prove the full index 8: the exhibited units give the lower bound, and the four-place reciprocity hyperplane gives the upper bound. No claim that the discovery unit candidates form a complete fundamental-unit basis is needed for this step.

In each quadratic extension the total ramified-place count is four, so the ambiguous class number equals the odd base class number. If a 2-class group were nonzero, its subgroup of elements of order two would be a nonzero F2-vector space with an involution; such an involution has a nonzero fixed vector because `(g−1)^2=0`. This would give an even ambiguous class group, a contradiction. Thus the two oddness deductions are valid and do not use a norm theorem for a noncyclic extension.

## Why actual Hilbert bases bypass approximate dyadic models

The important part of `reconstruct_raw66.gp` is `certify_actual_component_lu`, together with `local_square_fp`. The model-based logarithms first propose a coordinate matrix R. A full column minor selects d candidate **actual global elements**, with d=[L_P:Q2]+2. Their Hilbert pairing is computed in the original number field at the actual prime, and it is nonsingular. Those d classes are therefore independent and form a basis of the entire actual local squareclass group, whose dimension is d.

The matrix minor merely prescribes the coordinate names for that actual basis. For each of the 66 elements, the program divides by its proposed product of basis elements and tests the result as a square in the same actual completion. The denominator is cleared by a rational square and an exact identity check before `nfislocalpower`. Thus every proposed column is certified in an actual basis. The approximate field models can have been used to discover the coordinate guesses without being used as the final proof of their interpretation.

The seven Hilbert ranks and all 462 multiplication-back checks are precisely the statements required. Fresh support inventory and the w=(1+s)/2 test bind the primes to the correct two K-places. This argument does **not** prove that an arbitrary stored allowed matrix has its intended image interpretation; actual source-to-basis checks must use the same basis. The global-foundation report correctly leaves local-image completeness to separate claims, and the companion source-image checks perform the needed matching. Therefore this qualification is a dependency boundary, not a remaining defect in RANK-09.
