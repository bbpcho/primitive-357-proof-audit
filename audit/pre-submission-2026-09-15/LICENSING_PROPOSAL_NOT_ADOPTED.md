# Proposed licensing decision — not yet adopted

This proposal does not grant a licence and has not been published to the repository.

## Author-owned material

A conventional option is MIT for the original computational source and CC BY 4.0 for the original manuscript and explanatory documentation. MIT permits reuse, modification and redistribution of covered code subject to preservation of its notice. CC BY 4.0 permits sharing and adaptation, including commercial reuse, with attribution and a record of changes. Both grants would apply only to rights held by Peter Chocian.

The corresponding notices would be:

- Original computational source: Copyright (c) 2026 Peter Chocian; distributed under the MIT License.
- Original manuscript and explanatory documentation: Copyright (c) 2026 Peter Chocian; licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ .

## Explicit exclusions

No project notice would relicense cited literature, copied third-party papers, the lucasvillagra/GFE-5p3 snapshot at e88f914c577ab6cf9a45e5cdd82c1993477fb423, other identified upstream software, or material whose rights are held by another party. Existing third-party licences continue to govern their components. Unlicensed upstream material requires separate permission or a distribution design that does not redistribute it.

The project's own licence choice and upstream redistribution permission are separate decisions. A public repository does not automatically supply either. The arXiv submission licence is also a separate choice made by the submitting author.
