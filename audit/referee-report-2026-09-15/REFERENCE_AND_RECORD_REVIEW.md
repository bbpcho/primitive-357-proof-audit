## F5: citations and classification

- **Raynaud locator is correct.** Corollary 3.4.4 appears on printed page **270** (PDF page 31 including its cover). It applies to Jordan–Hölder factors of a finite commutative group scheme killed by a power of p whose generic fibre extends to a finite flat scheme over the valuation ring. It bounds fundamental-character exponents between 0 and the absolute ramification index e. For the manuscript's unramified place above 7, e=1. Passing to the strict henselization gives the inertia statement; a scalar character of the full local Galois group is Frobenius-invariant, so its tame inertia order divides 49−1 and its level divides two. Thus the displayed exponent r₀+7r₁ with rᵢ∈{0,1} is consistent with this citation. The finite-flat premise is explicitly supplied at manuscript lines 1013–1015 by PVT Theorem 2.1(4), whose statement does not impose irreducibility. The present check confirms the citation/application boundary, not a new proof of PVT's theorem. [Original Raynaud paper, p.270](https://www.numdam.org/article/BSMF_1974__102__241_0.pdf#page=31).
- **Breuil–Diamond is PVT's actual reference.** PVT v1 Theorem 7.8's proof explicitly cites BD14 for Hilbert level lowering. Current manuscript lines 1017–1021 already invoke that specialized theorem and say its proof cites Breuil–Diamond. The bibliography at 1771–1776 identifies the same article and DOI. Replacing it with a guessed Fujiwara/Jarvis/Rajaei citation would be unwarranted. [PVT v1, Theorem 7.8 and proof](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17845v1#S7.SS2); [PVT Theorem 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17845v1#S2.SS3).
- **Dembélé–Voight is already corrected.** Manuscript lines 1784–1794 give the published chapter, 2013, pp.135–198, DOI, and separately identify the corrected authors' version used for theorem numbering. These details match the [publisher's record](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-0348-0618-3_4) and [authors' corrected version](https://jvoight.github.io/articles/hmf-crm-bcn-053024.pdf).

The 60-second calculator limit is recorded in the archive README line 3 and `INPUT_INDEX.json`, and is confirmed by the [official calculator page](https://magma.maths.usyd.edu.au/calc/). The Section 7 job reports 26.149 seconds; all seven recorded jobs took less than 60 seconds individually. The current paper at lines 1299–1305 names the calculator/version and separates software trust from independent implementations, but does not print the time limit or these flags. Adding them would improve visibility. Their omission does **not** leave the current argument assuming that an arbitrary finite-index subgroup is the full group: lines 622–647 explicitly import Dahmen–Siksek Lemma 7.3/full rational and twisted groups, and lines 1294–1298 preserve that dependency separately from the quadratic-field Kummer step.
