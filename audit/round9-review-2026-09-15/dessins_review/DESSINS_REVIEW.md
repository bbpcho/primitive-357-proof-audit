# Exact review of report §9.1

Date: 2026-09-15. This is a fresh bounded check of the supplied counting script and the accompanying permutation-group arguments. It does not rerun the paper's canonical function-field certificate or validate numerical root tracking.

## Outcome

The reported counts are correct:

* There are **1,591,439,705,856,000** ordered triples in `S15` with product one and the ordered cycle types `(5^3, 3^5, 7^2 1)`.
* They give **1217** simultaneous `S15`-conjugacy classes, hence 1217 isomorphism classes of connected complex Belyi covers of the line with its branch values labelled `0,1,infinity`. All have genus 3.
* In either Fano 15-point action of `A7`, the required triples number **5040**. They form **two** simultaneous `A7`-conjugacy classes of size 2520, each consisting of generating triples. Those classes remain distinct under `S15` conjugacy.
* The Fano image of `A7` has centralizer of order **1** and normalizer of order **2520** in `S15`; thus it is self-normalizing.

The last sentence of §9.1, **“the monodromy group does”**, needs qualification. The passport together with monodromy group `A7` in this action determines **an unordered pair of covers**, not a chosen cover. It determines a single class only after also identifying the two covers under the nontrivial conjugation of `Q(sqrt(-7))/Q`. Suggested replacement:

> The passport does not determine the cover. Adding the Fano action of A7 narrows it to the two conjugate Fano covers; selecting C+ still requires the orientation or an exact identification.

Also, “conjugate over K7” is potentially misleading: the exchange is **under the nontrivial automorphism of `K7/Q`, namely `s -> -s`**, not under an automorphism fixing `K7`. The non-isomorphism established here is non-isomorphism **as covers of the labelled t-line**; no assertion about the abstract curves after forgetting t is needed or made.

## Why division by 15! is justified

Let `G` be generated by a triple of the prescribed types.

1. A `G`-orbit is a union of 5-cycles of the first generator and a union of 3-cycles of the second. Its size is therefore divisible by both 5 and 3. In degree 15 there can only be one orbit.
2. A nontrivial block system for this transitive action has 3 blocks of size 5 or 5 blocks of size 3. An element of order 7 acts trivially on fewer than 7 blocks, so it must stabilize each block; it then acts trivially within each block, also of size less than 7. This contradicts its type `7^2 1`. Hence the action is primitive.
3. Trivial centralizer follows even without the primitivity step. An element commuting with the triple must preserve the unique fixed point of its `7^2 1` member. An element centralizing a transitive group and fixing one point fixes every point. Thus every simultaneous conjugacy orbit has size `15!`.
4. The branch generators have 3, 5, and 3 cycles. The Euler characteristic is `3+5+3-15=-4`, so the covering curve has genus 3. The resulting ramification deficit is 34, as in Riemann–Hurwitz.

These arguments apply to every counted triple, not merely to selected examples.

## Independent A7, Fano-action, and normalizer checks

The independent program constructs a Fano plane directly from the nonzero vectors of `F2^3`, with lines `{u,v,u+v}`. Its `S7` orbit has 30 elements; its two `A7` orbits have 15 each. Both induced degree-15 actions are faithful, and point stabilizers have order 168.

There are 504 single 5-cycles and 70 single 3-cycles in the seven-letter `A7`. Direct enumeration finds 5040 ordered pairs whose product is a 7-cycle. They split into exactly two simultaneous `A7`-conjugacy orbits of size 2520. Actual multiplication closure of one representative from each orbit yields all 2520 elements of `A7`; consequently **every** enumerated triple generates `A7`. An odd permutation interchanges these two triple classes and also interchanges the two Fano-plane orbits.

Write `Ai/Tj` for triple class `j` acting through Fano action `i`. The counts of equivariant bijections of the 15 sheets are:

| from / to | A0/T0 | A0/T1 | A1/T0 | A1/T1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A0/T0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| A0/T1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| A1/T0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| A1/T1 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

This test is exhaustive: for a transitive action, a simultaneous intertwiner is forced by the image of one sheet. The program tries all 15 possible images, propagates along both generators and their inverses, and checks bijectivity and both exact intertwining identities. In particular, the two triples in a fixed Fano action remain non-isomorphic as covers, while the same septic triple through the two actions gives the two different classes.

For a further direct normalizer check, any element normalizing the degree-15 `A7` image carries a fixed generating pair into one of the enumerated 5040 pairs: the cycle distribution verifies that type `5^3` comes exactly from single 5-cycles and type `3^5` exactly from single 3-cycles. The program counts intertwiners from that fixed pair to **every** such possible image pair. The total is 2520, so the normalizer is the image itself. This avoids relying on a classification of `Aut(A7)` or merely assuming that maximality of a point stabilizer proves self-normalization in `S15`.

The claim that a point stabilizer is maximal also follows here: a proper intervening subgroup would have index 3 or 5 in `A7`; simplicity of `A7` would make the corresponding action faithful, impossible since `2520 > 5!`. Maximality by itself is not a general proof that a permutation group is self-normalizing in the ambient symmetric group; the separate normalizer computation supplies that assertion.

The full degree-15 cycle distribution is recorded in `independent_results.json` and agrees with §9.2: identity 1, `2^6 1^3` 105, `3^4 1^3` 280, `3^5` 70, `4^3 2 1` 630, `5^3` 504, `6^2 3` 210, and `7^2 1` 720.

## Connection with the paper and limits

The current manuscript at repository commit `4983db9` defines the Fano invariant and the two orbit resolvents in `paper/manuscript.tex`, lines 804–825. It explicitly says the two orbit polynomials are conjugate and fixes a Vandermonde orientation before naming `R+`. Its exact canonical forward and inverse identification is separate, at lines 853–868.

For the septic polynomial `Phi(T)=15T^7-35T^6+21T^5`, the identity `Phi'(T)=105 T^4 (T-1)^2`, together with `Phi(0)=0`, `Phi(1)=1`, gives the seven-sheet passport used above. Reading that septic triple through the two Fano actions therefore gives precisely the two classes in the table. The manuscript's exact orbit definition `R+=(U+sW)/2` and its conjugate account for the arithmetic exchange `s -> -s`; the finite permutation count alone does not choose which class receives the plus label.

The original false passport-identification inference is correctly withdrawn in §9.1. The counting work strengthens the explanation of why it was false, but does **not** replace the paper's exact identification, prove a numerical monodromy computation rigorous, or discharge the current publication/replay availability issue. This review establishes the count for the explicit Fano 15-point action; it does not undertake an additional abstract classification of all degree-15 representations of `A7`.
