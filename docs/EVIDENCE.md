# Evidence and verification boundary

The release distinguishes exact input data, recorded output, replayable computations, and independently checked mathematical implications. Hash equality identifies bytes; it does not prove the statement written in those bytes. A successful historical replay is used only for its documented scope.

## Immutable inputs and adopted checks

The new release preserves the V3, p=5 and rank-closure repositories under separate roots:

- `evidence/v3/repository/`
- `evidence/p5/repository/`
- `evidence/rank/repository/`

The audit sources and reports are carried under `audit/work/`, with their dependency indexes. Runtime installations and untracked caches are not evidence inputs. Replays use declared paths in the extracted release and a separate output directory.

The earlier class/unit, finite-Jacobian, saturation, and full logarithm checks supply the remaining premises.

The absent historical p=7 cycle-3 worker is replaced by `reconstruct_p7_witness_unramified.py`, its exact contact input binding, finite quotient check and companion full-component square/source proofs. The replacement does not claim to reproduce an unavailable program byte for byte.

A final release decision records which replays were actually executed.

## Scope of the group statements

Rank four proves that the original point subgroup H0 has finite index. Its 2-saturation gives odd index. The 5-saturated group is H1, which contains the branch-division class E. It is H1 that has index coprime to 400 and covers the finite sieve coefficients. The logarithmic annihilator already extends from H0 by finite index. See [the correction notice](H0_H1_CORRIGENDUM.md).

A published theorem or authenticated transcript is not silently promoted to a fresh independent computation. Conversely, a formerly missing interface is no longer listed as open once the replacement proof and its hypotheses have been independently checked.

## Asset identity

The new asset has a new basename and release tag. Its exact byte size, SHA-256 and final verification results are recorded in the machine-readable indexes generated at sealing. Earlier archives and audit bundles retain their identities, including records of failed attempts and superseded arguments. The new correction notices explain the changes without overwriting that history.
