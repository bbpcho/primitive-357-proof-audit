# Correction to the subgroup notation in the rank audit

The 14 September audit addendum initially denoted the subgroup generated by the four point differences by H, then attributed the coprime-to 400 index conclusion to H. That last attribution is corrected as follows.

Write H0=⟨D2,D3,D4,D5⟩ and H1=⟨D2,E,D4,D5⟩, with 5E=D3+D4+3D5. The verified 2-saturation gives H0 odd index. The relation gives H0⊆H1. The verified 5-saturation applies to H1, so gcd([J(K):H1],400)=1. Thus H1, rather than the original H0, supplies the coefficient coverage modulo 400. The original point subgroup H0 has finite index and remains sufficient for extending its characteristic-zero logarithmic annihilator to all of J(K).

This corrects the final saturation paragraph of `rank_implication_addendum.md` and any reading of the review that assigns 5-saturation to the original four point differences. The computed finite sieve already uses the E basis on the 5-primary side. Its arithmetic and the rank proof are unchanged. The revised manuscript names both subgroups explicitly.
