# Mordell--Weil rank gap: completion contract

## The question to be settled

The manuscript uses a rank-four Mordell--Weil conclusion and downstream
saturation/local-log information in the terminal rational-point argument.
The evidence tree contains computations supporting that conclusion, including
a Selmer character-projector certificate, finite-field Kummer witnesses, two
split-23 logarithm branches, and the later B124/D4 reconstruction.  The open
audit question is whether those pieces form a complete proof from stated
inputs, rather than a consistent collection of downstream records.

This is primarily a provenance-and-logic gap, though resolving it may reveal a
mathematical correction.  Passing programs alone is not enough: the audit must
show why the computed group controls every rational divisor class used by the
argument.

## Current boundary

The saturation computations and both split-23 logarithm reconstructions are
complete at the current V3/post-V3 evidence boundary.  In particular, the
B124 tensor lift, the two computations of 75D, the formal-chart decoder, and
the D4 logarithm are no longer missing.  These stages should be replayed as
regression checks, not listed as unsolved mathematics.

The remaining rank gap is the clean proof of the Mordell--Weil upper bound and
the exact logical composition that turns the four known independent classes,
the completed saturation data, and the Selmer calculation into the group
statement used by the sieve and Chabauty argument.

## Required theorem statement

Before computation, the rank audit must name:

- the curve and base field, with exact model and field embedding conventions;
- the Jacobian and the four displayed divisor classes;
- the subgroup generated by those classes;
- the claimed rank, torsion, and index/saturation statement;
- the exact implication needed by the Mordell--Weil sieve and the split-prime
  Chabauty/Siksek calculation.

No verifier should infer a stronger group statement from a rank matrix alone.

## Mandatory gates

### 1. Exact model and point gate

- Reconstruct the curve from the printed or canonical exact coefficients.
- Check smoothness and genus.
- Check every displayed point and divisor.
- Bind both primes above 23 by explicit residue embeddings, not by list order.

### 2. Rank lower bound

- Compute an exact or rigorously certified independence witness for the four
  displayed classes.
- Record the finite quotient, prime, coordinate convention, matrix, and a unit
  minor proving rank four.
- Independently recompute that witness from the curve and divisor inputs.

### 3. Rank upper bound

- Reconstruct the relevant Selmer group or other upper-bound object from its
  defining local conditions.
- Replay the character/projector calculation from the actual local data.
- Include the complete p=5 obstruction dependency closure, not just the two
  final hard-coded signs.
- Prove that the computed object bounds the stated Mordell--Weil rank; identify
  every general theorem used and verify its hypotheses.

### 4. Torsion and saturation — completed calculation, composition retained

- Determine the torsion subgroup used by the argument.
- Prove the required index statement for the displayed rank-four subgroup.
- Separate 2-saturation, odd-prime saturation, and any inference made from
  reduction maps.
- Retain the independent finite-field Kummer witness, while making clear which
  part of generation it proves and which parts remain imported.

### 5. Global logarithm reconstruction — completed regression gate

- Recompute all four logarithm columns at both split-23 places from their
  upstream divisor classes.
- For the D4 column, include and replay the B124 graded-lattice producer,
  tensor lift, two 75D class computations, formal-chart decoder, and
  integration step.
- Do not make a saved first-log cache the sole load-bearing source.
- Record precision loss, unit pivots, ball containment, and a higher-precision
  stability replay.

### 6. Downstream-use gate

- Verify the rank of the combined logarithm matrix and the construction of its
  annihilator lattice.
- Check that the local uniqueness or sieve theorem is applied with its exact
  hypotheses.
- Ensure the paper does not turn a finite-index statement into equality unless
  equality has actually been proved.
- Trace each final rational-point exclusion back to the rank/saturation result
  it consumes.

### 7. Independence and clean extraction

- Run the reconstruction in an extraction where the original workspace is
  inaccessible.
- Reject undeclared absolute paths, implicit caches, symlinks, and generated
  files not covered by a manifest.
- Require a second auditor to reconstruct the mathematical invariants without
  reading the first auditor's conclusion.

## Acceptance output

A successful audit should produce:

1. a human-readable theorem-and-proof report;
2. a machine-readable decision document;
3. exact replay logs with software versions and elapsed boundaries;
4. a strict, sorted, self-excluding SHA-256 manifest;
5. a dependency graph from primary inputs to the final rank statement;
6. a manuscript patch that cites this exact certificate and makes no broader
   claim.

If any gate fails, the correct output is a no-go record identifying the first
failed implication.  Failure evidence is progress: it narrows the theorem
that still needs proving.

## Definition of done

The rank gap is closed only when both independent decisions are affirmative,
their manifests replay, the clean reconstruction uses no undeclared cache,
and the paper's statement is no stronger than the verified conclusion.
